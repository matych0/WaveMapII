## Working agreement
- Do not modify files unless I explicitly ask for a change.
- Propose changes first (describe what and why), then wait for my approval.
- Make the smallest change that solves the stated problem; no refactoring,
  renaming, reformatting or "while I'm here" fixes.
- Do not modify existing utility functions unless I say so.
- Do not commit, push, or install packages.
- If something looks wrong outside the scope of the request, tell me
  instead of fixing it.
